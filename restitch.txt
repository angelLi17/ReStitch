If you have an old T-shirt lying around, you can easily upcycle it into a simple and eco-friendly tote bag—no sewing required. 

Start by laying the shirt flat and smoothing out any wrinkles. 

Cut off the sleeves, following the seam or slightly inside it, to create the handles. 

Then, cut a wide U-shape around the neckline to form the opening of your tote. 

Decide how deep you want the bag to be, and if necessary, trim off some length from the bottom.

Next, create a fringe along the bottom edge by cutting vertical slits about 3 to 4 inches long and roughly an inch apart. 
You’ll end up with a row of strips from both the front and back layers of the shirt.
 Tie each front strip to its matching back strip in a tight double knot. 
For a more secure base, you can tie the strips diagonally—each front strip to the back strip beside it—to help close any gaps between knots. 
If you'd rather sew for added durability, flip the shirt inside out and sew a straight line across the bottom instead of tying the fringe. 
You can also hem the neckline and armholes for a cleaner finish. 
Feel free to personalize your tote with fabric paint, iron-on patches, or even add a pocket using fabric scraps. It's a fun and sustainable way to give an old shirt new life.
